Shipping

Last updated October 26, 2026

We sell our finds across several marketplaces — eBay, Poshmark, Mercari, Depop, Vinted, Whatnot, and Facebook Marketplace. Every order is placed and fulfilled through the marketplace you buy on, so checkout, payment, and tracking all stay on that platform. Here's how we ship, everywhere we sell.

Handling time

We aim to ship every order within 1 business day of purchase — most orders go out the same day.

Carrier

Most packages ship via USPS. Larger or heavier items may ship via a different carrier when that's the better fit for the item.

Cost

Our standard shipping rate is a flat $7.99 per item for delivery within the United States. Because every order checks out on the marketplace you buy through, that marketplace's shipping rate can differ — some of our listings ship free while we test shipping options. The exact cost is always shown at checkout before you pay.

Tracking

A tracking number is provided on every order through the marketplace you purchased from, so you can follow your package the whole way.

International orders

Where international shipping is offered, shipping cost, customs, and any import fees are the buyer's responsibility and are shown at checkout.

Questions

Message us through the marketplace you purchased from, or reach us directly at support@movingmountains.community.
