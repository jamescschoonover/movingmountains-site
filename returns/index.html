Policies

Returns & Refunds

Last updated October 13, 2026

We sell our finds across several marketplaces — eBay, Poshmark, Mercari, Depop, Vinted, Whatnot, and Facebook Marketplace. Every purchase is completed on the marketplace you buy through, so that marketplace's own buyer protection applies to your order in addition to the policy below. We honor this return policy on every order, everywhere we sell.

30-day returns

We accept returns within 30 days of delivery. To start a return, use the return/request process on the marketplace you purchased from (eBay, Poshmark, Mercari, Depop, Vinted, Whatnot, or Facebook) — that keeps your order, payment, and refund all on one platform.

Condition

Items should be returned in the same condition they were received, with any original tags or packaging included where applicable. We sell pre-owned and vintage goods, so an item's listed condition (noted in the listing description) is what we compare a return against — not "new."

Refunds

Once we receive the returned item and confirm it matches the condition it was sent in, we issue a full refund to your original payment method through the marketplace you purchased from.

Return shipping

For returns shipped from within the United States, we cover the return shipping cost. For international orders, return shipping is the buyer's responsibility.

Questions

Message us through the marketplace you purchased from, or reach us directly at support@movingmountains.community.
