Common questions

Is Lamplight really free?
Yes, and it always will be. There's an optional way to support the people who build it in Settings, but nothing is ever locked behind it.

Does Lamplight need an internet connection?
No. The Bible text, translations, and commentaries are all built into the app itself.

Is my data private?
Yes — see our Privacy Policy. Lamplight collects nothing about you or what you read; the only thing it sends is an anonymous crash report if the app breaks.
