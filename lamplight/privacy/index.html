Lamplight

"Your word is a lamp to my feet and a light to my path." — Psalm 119:105

In short: we don't collect anything about you or what you read. The only thing the app ever sends is an anonymous crash report if it breaks, so we can fix it.

Data collection

Lamplight does not collect, sell, or share any personal information.

  • No account. Lamplight has no sign-up, sign-in, or user profile.
  • No analytics, advertising, or tracking. We don't track what you read, how long you read, or that you use the app at all, and nothing is used to track you across other apps or websites.
  • Your reading stays on your device. The Bible text, translations, and commentaries are bundled with the app. Reading, searching, bookmarking, highlighting, and notes all happen entirely on your device.
  • Crash reports. If the app crashes or hits an error, it sends a diagnostic report to Sentry (our crash-reporting service): the error and where in the app's code it happened, plus technical details such as device model, operating-system version, and app version. Reports carry no name, email, account, or other identifier of you, and are used only to find and fix bugs.

What's stored, and where

The following preferences are saved only on your device, and are never transmitted anywhere:

  • Your bookmarked verses
  • Your highlighted verses and their colors
  • Your appearance setting (Light, Dark, or Follow Device)
  • Your text-size preference
  • Your notes
  • Your reading-plan progress and last-read chapter
  • Your translation and reminder-time choices

Deleting the app deletes this data. We have no copy of it.

Third parties

  • Sentry receives the anonymous crash reports described above.
  • Expo (the platform Lamplight is built on) delivers app updates; when the app checks for one, it sends only technical details such as the app version and platform.
  • Apple runs the App Store and processes any optional donation.

Daily verse reminders are scheduled on your device, and the "Listen" feature uses your device's own built-in text-to-speech; neither sends anything to us.

In-app donations

If you choose to support Lamplight through an optional in-app donation, that payment is processed entirely by Apple's own In-App Purchase system. Lamplight never sees or stores your payment information.

Children's privacy

Lamplight collects no personal information from anyone, including children. The app displays public-domain Bible text and commentary and has no chat, messaging, or user-generated content.
